Company Announcement

Example Pet Foods, Inc. of Springfield, Illinois is voluntarily recalling three lots of Example Kitchen Chicken Recipe Dry Dog Food because they may be contaminated with Salmonella. The affected bags carry UPC 0 12345 67891 2 on the back panel.

The recalled products were distributed to retail stores in Illinois, Indiana and Wisconsin and sold online.
