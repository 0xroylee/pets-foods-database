FDA Advises Pet Owners Not to Feed Certain Lots of Example Kitchen Dog Food

October 29, 2026

The U.S. Food and Drug Administration is advising pet owners not to feed lots 2026A1 and 2026A2 of Example Kitchen Chicken Recipe Dry Dog Food (UPC 0 12345 67890 5), which may contain Salmonella.
